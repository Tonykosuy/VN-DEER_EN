💡 Survival Tips for Flood/Storm ▼
🌊 When flood waters rise
  • Move to higher floors or roof
  • Bring clean water and flashlight
  • Do not try to swim through fast currents
  • Call rescue: 113 or 114
⚡ Electrical safety during storms
  • Turn off circuit breakers when water enters
  • Do not touch electrical appliances with wet hands
  • Stay away from electric poles, broken wires
  • Do not use phone while charging
🏥 First Aid & Basic Medical
  • Drink boiled water to avoid digestive diseases
  • Wash hands frequently with soap
  • Treat wounds with 70-degree alcohol
  • Move hypothermic people to warm place

🛟 Flood Survival Tips
🏠 When indoors
  • Move to higher floors, bring water and flashlight
  • Turn off electricity and gas to avoid danger
  • Save emergency numbers: 113, 114, 115
🌊 Moving through flood water
  • Use a stick to check for deep holes
  • Do not cross fast-flowing water
  • Keep children and elderly in the center of the group
⚠️ Danger Warning
  • Do not swim across flood waters
  • Stay away from fallen power poles and lines
  • Do not eat food soaked in flood water
